# Copy for a launch kit

Write from the person's chair. Name what they can now get done and the pain they no longer carry. Use plain task verbs. Cut hype, throat-clearing, internal terms, and claims the footage does not prove.

## Lead

Use one or two short sentences under the feature name. Start with better work, not the mechanism. When relevant, the second sentence gives the split: the product does the legwork; the person decides.

Good:

> Move your work forward right in the conversation, without reading long replies or switching apps. Bonsai brings up the right screen, and you make the call.

> Find time together without a chain of scheduling messages. Compare the options and choose what works.

A tour of a live screen, its buttons, or its save behavior is not a value lead. Ask: who ends up better off, and how? If three sentences in a row make the system the subject, rewrite.

## Task labels

Use “Plan your day”, “Find a time to meet”, or “Set a priority”. Avoid “Open the panel”, “Click the dropdown”, test subjects, and people's names. Order previews by the tasks people reach for first.

Watch the whole clip against its label. “Schedule a meeting” must show that task, not only opening an existing event. If it does not, re-record the promised flow. Do not hide the mismatch with a new label.

## Never narrate the UI

This rule belongs in every helper brief too. No tagline, helper line, caption, or label that restates the screen. Text is data, a task label, or the value lead. Speed labels on compressed waits are required recording truth, not explanatory captions.

No audience, feature flag, merge state, recording date, how-it-works section, or status footer. Put provenance in the README. An explicitly requested coming-soon unit gets only a plain placeholder. Do not invent a capability for it.

Do not add a numbered step strip or repeat the task label in a helper paragraph. The preview carries the detail. Keep any requested announcement draft short: value lead, link, one preview. A draft never authorizes posting.
