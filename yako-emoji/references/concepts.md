# Complete Yako concept library

These are the 20 distinct concepts created in the originating team chat. Each includes a portable example and a concise recipe. Names are working names unless the user chose them. Follow a new request over an earlier recipe. GIF examples can be decoded to inspect frames if the image-reference tool requires PNG input. They demonstrate the intended scene; do not assume every animation was explicitly endorsed.

## 1. Smooth brain / simplify

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-smooth-brain.gif)

**Meaning:** simplifying a complex concept, not insulting someone's intelligence. Head-and-torso Yako with a large visible pink cartoon brain. The brain starts deeply wrinkled, then visibly smooths into a glossy rounded shape, ending with a satisfied “aha” expression. Keep the transformation prominent at small size.

**Revision lesson:** the first full-body version was too small and indistinct. The close-up and exaggerated wrinkles-to-smooth transition became the desired direction. The brain is specific to this concept; ordinary Yako has cream hair.

## 2. Brain dump / context dump

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-brain-dump.gif)

Yako drives a chunky orange dump truck, near hoof resting on the open window sill, head turned backward toward the cargo bed. The bed tips, dumps a bunch of papers, then lowers. Show a large face in the cab, a clear rear hinge, papers visibly exiting the container and a brief pause after dumping. The original wording mentioned lowering the container; the implemented sequence tips it to discharge and lowers it afterward.

## 3. Touch grass

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-touch-grass.gif)

Close-up Yako reaches down to a bright green patch of grass, pats it, closes his eyes with a contented smile and lifts his hoof to reset. Keep the grassy patch and contact large and obvious. Use a calm natural arm motion and a stable body.

## 4. Let me cook

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-let-me-cook.gif)

Confident chef Yako in a white toque and jacket with red neckerchief, holding a dark frying pan with a tall orange-yellow flambé flare. A brief wink and flickering fire communicate confidence. Preserve believable hoof grip and keep fire above the pan. The original output filenames used “let-him-cook”; the user's phrase was “let me cook.”

## 5. Side quest

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-side-quest.gif)

Fantasy-adventurer Yako in a teal tunic and short red cape carries a silver sword with gold crossguard. A fork in the road has a smaller branch marked with a simple exclamation-point sign. Yako takes that branch. Keep sword, fork and chosen direction clear. A still can show the committed step onto the side path.

## 6. Low-hanging fruit

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-low-hanging-fruit.gif)

Yako plucks a large red apple from a branch hanging ridiculously close to the ground, then lifts it. The humor is the absurdly low fruit, so make that spatial relationship unambiguous. Keep the hoof, apple and branch connected correctly through the picking action.

## 7. Punt

[Current still](../assets/examples/yako-punt.png)

Yako wears a royal-blue sports jersey with white neck/sleeve trim, balances on one planted leg and kicks an American football upward. One leg extended in a clear follow-through, arms spread for balance, ball close to the hoof with short motion strokes. Preserve cute face and costume.

**Revision lesson:** the user explicitly disliked the earlier animation while liking Yako and his outfit, and requested a still. Do not reuse the detached rotating-leg implementation as the default.

## 8. Coffee

[Animation](../assets/examples/yako-coffee.gif)

Large teal coffee mug, sleepy half-closed eyes, a big sip, then comically wide eyes and caffeinated energy. Intended both for needing coffee and being over-caffeinated. Use clear sleepy → sip → wired states; keep mug style, hoof grip and character scale consistent. Keep the face cute even at the wired extreme.

## 9. Multi-threading / spider Yako

[Current still](../assets/examples/yako-multithreading.png)

Yako has a low horizontal spider body, cute face at the front, rounded furry abdomen behind, and **exactly eight** bent furry legs—four per side. Each hoof operates its own laptop: **eight laptops**, arranged on one implied horizontal surface in an oval around him. Raised knees bend outward and down. Frontmost two laptops show plain gray lid backs; their displays face Yako.

**Revision lessons:** the user first specified many laptops, then disliked the six-arm/three-laptop animation. The eventual spider version improved from an upright fan of limbs to a grounded spider crouch. Correct front laptop perspective matters. `yak-threaded` was suggested but not explicitly selected.

## 10. Meat proxy

[Current still](../assets/examples/yako-meat-proxy.png)

The AI controls Yako's physical actions. Claude's orange irregular starburst icon sits above Yako's forelock, with two small arms gripping and lifting two cream hair tufts like Remy steering Linguini. Yako has a cute happily vacant face and forward-reaching hooves. Keep the hair visibly connected to the forelock and gripped by the icon's hands.

**Revision lessons:** the initial AI-chip marionette animation was disliked. A robot with a game controller improved Yako, but the user disliked the robot/device. The hair-pulling Claude icon was the latest direction. Do not import the controller or strings into it. Claude branding is specific to this requested concept.

## 11. Spider-Pig scene

[Current still](../assets/examples/yako-spider-pig.png)

Happy Yako in a white polo holds a plump upside-down pink pig overhead. Preserve round face, cream hair/beard, big upward-looking eyes and open smile with pink tongue. The pig faces **left**, with a snout at far left, eyes beneath it, drooping ears, a little cream tuft, curly tail at right and upward legs with blue-gray hooves.

**Revision lessons:** user selected the cute centered Yako from one version and the left-facing pig from another. Avoid the stretched human-toothy Yako from the rejected version. The brief request for human Homer holding Yako produced no deliverable and was superseded; it is not the current example. This concept is a still.

## 12. Yako supreme / agent army

[Current still](../assets/examples/yako-supreme.png)

User-selected name: **`yako-supreme`**, a Taco Supreme pun. Yako commands six cyan robot agents arranged in two rows, front row saluting. Oversized olive military peaked cap with gold star/braid and black visor, determined brows and smug little smile. One hoof points forward giving orders; other arm spreads outward. Keep robot shapes bold and distinct.

**Revision lessons:** began as an orchestra-conductor metaphor with a baton, then gained the military hat. The final revision removed the baton in favor of a pointing hoof. A proper simplified hoof gesture is preferable to human fingers. Keep this concept distinct from spider multi-threading.

## 13. Popcorn / watching the drama

[Still](../assets/examples/yako-popcorn.png)

Yako wears white cardboard 3D glasses with red/cyan lenses. One hoof brings popcorn to his open mouth while the other hugs a red-and-white striped bucket overflowing with yellow popcorn. Big excited eyes remain visible through the colored lenses. Close-up, one or two loose kernels, no theater background required. User corrected the original animation request to a still.

## 14. Absolute cinema

[Latest still](../assets/examples/yako-absolute-cinema.png)

Match the original Scorsese pose: Yako leans slightly back in a light-gray upholstered armchair, elbows spread wide, forearms upright, both cloven hooves raised above his ears. Calm near-neutral expression and more open eyes; black jacket and dark buttoned shirt. **Neutral black-and-white/grayscale**, transparent outside the Yako-and-chair silhouette.

**Revision lessons:** replace all human-like hands with two-lobed hooves. Earlier sleepy smile and lower hands were revised after inspecting the original. Color was later changed to black-and-white; the chair was then added. No caption was requested.

## 15. Painter / afro Yako

[Still](../assets/examples/yako-painter.png)

Big rounded fluffy brown afro, little cream forelock and horns still visible, relaxed friendly face, light-blue rolled-sleeve shirt. One hoof holds a wooden palette with bold paint blobs; the other holds a brush touching a canvas on an easel. Canvas contains a simple lake, mountains and evergreen trees. Keep Yako and the painting readable together; no extra studio scenery.

## 16. Yak cry

[Animation](../assets/examples/yak-cry.gif) · [Source sheet](../assets/yak-cry-sheet.png)

User-selected name: **`yak-cry`**. Yako sits on his bottom with two legs forward, big cloven hoof soles facing viewer, arms down beside hips. Sad squeezed-shut eyes, raised inner brows and open wailing mouth. Two broad cyan waterfalls pour from his eyes into splashes beside his feet. Flowing white streaks travel downward while splashes change; little sobbing squash, stable feet.

Four registered frames at 0.14 seconds each produced the bundled 0.56-second loop. See [animation.md](animation.md) for assembly. Timing is an example, not a requirement for every crying animation. A local renamed copy was called `yak-sad`; that is not a separate concept.

## 17. Yak smash

[Still](../assets/examples/yak-smash.png)

User-selected name: **`yak-smash`**. Hulk-like green Yako, pale-lime hair/chest, tan horns, exaggerated muscular furry shoulders/forearms and torn purple shorts. Low squat, both huge dark-green cloven hooves strike a small cracked stone slab. Bold yellow impact burst and a few rock chunks. Keep his open roaring expression funny and cute, with no human fists.

## 18. Yako cursed

[Still](../assets/examples/yako-cursed.png)

User-selected name: **`yako-cursed`**. Mischievous witch Yako, oversized crooked purple hat with buckle, purple cloak collar, impish smile. One hoof stirs a black cauldron of bright lime bubbling potion; other holds a tiny stitched burlap Yako doll with horns, X eyes and an oversized round-headed pin. Clearly a fabric toy. Keep face, doll and potion visible; a single curled steam puff is enough.

## 19. Yako boomer

[Still](../assets/examples/yako-boomer.png)

User-selected name: **`yako-boomer`**. Adorable elderly Yako with fluffy silver-white forelock/beard, bushy white eyebrows, a few smile lines and round reading glasses low on his muzzle. Cozy burgundy cardigan, slight stoop, both cloven hooves on a wooden walking cane. Lovable, gently skeptical expression; retain the round cute face.

## 20. How do you do, fellow kids?

[Still](../assets/examples/yako-fellow-kids.png)

Backward bright red cap between horns, unzipped red hoodie, gray T-shirt reading **MUSIC BAND**, skateboard over viewer-left shoulder held by its truck with one cloven hoof. Dark deck underside with a few colorful stickers and cream wheels. Slight three-quarter pose, sideways glance, raised brows and awkwardly eager open smile. Match the supplied meme's clothing and pose while retaining Yako's face.

Shirt lettering is part of this meme and is an intentional exception to the usual no-text default. Keep the board's orientation and shoulder/hoof contact believable.
